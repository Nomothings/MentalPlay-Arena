### 1. **Anhedonia**
**Characteristics:** Lack of interest and pleasure in activities, difficulty experiencing joy.

### 2. **Anxiousness**
**Characteristics:** Excessive worry, nervousness, anticipating the worst outcomes.

**Clues:** Seeks attention, exaggerated behavior, wishes to be noticed.

### 4. **Callousness**
**Characteristics:** Lack of empathy, insensitivity to others' emotions.
**Clues:** "Doesn't care about others", "No reaction to family's distress", "Indifferent attitude".

### 5. **Deceitfulness**
**Characteristics:** Tendency to conceal facts, deceive, mislead others.

### 6. **Depressivity**

**Clues:** "I am useless", "Life is meaningless", "Feels worthless".

### 7. **Distractibility**
**Characteristics:** Difficulty sustaining attention, easily distracted.
**Clues:** "Difficulty concentrating", "Easily interrupted while doing things".

### 8. **Eccentricity**
**Characteristics:** Odd or unconventional behavior, expression, or thought patterns.

### 9. **Emotional Lability**
**Characteristics:** Rapid and significant mood swings.

### 10. **Grandiosity**

**Clues:** "I am special", "Others are inferior to me".

### 16. **Perceptual Dysregulation**

### 17. **Perseveration**

### 20. **Risk Taking**

### 21. **Separation Insecurity**

**Clues:** "Can't stand being alone", "Afraid others will leave".

### 22. **Submissiveness**

### 23. **Suspiciousness**
**Characteristics:** Distrust of others, suspicion of others' motives.
**Clues:** "Others are targeting me", "Doubtful of what others say".

### 24. **Unusual Beliefs & Experiences**
**Characteristics:** Possesses peculiar beliefs or experiences not reaching psychotic intensity.
**Clues:** Supernatural experiences, peculiar explanations, feelings others don't understand.

### 25. **Withdrawal**
