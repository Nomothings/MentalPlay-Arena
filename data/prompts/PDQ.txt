**Characteristics:** Paranoid personality disorder, also known as delusional personality disorder, is characterized by suspicion and paranoia. ### 2. Schizoid Trait
**Characteristics:** Individuals with schizoid personality disorder are socially isolated, emotionally cold, lack interest and ability in social interaction, thus unable to establish intimate relationships, leading to difficulties in social adaptation.
**Clues:** Lack of interest in social activities, extreme social isolation, minimal emotional expression, very low level of social activity.

### 3. Schizotypal Trait
**Characteristics:** Symptoms of schizotypal personality disorder are more severe than those of schizoid personality disorder. In addition to social isolation and detachment, they include perceptual distortions and thought disorder similar to schizophrenia. Manifestations include odd beliefs, behaviors, and appearance, affective flattening, deficits in interpersonal relationships, introversion, stiff expression, lack of empathy, and lack of interest in sexual activity with the opposite sex.  
**Clues:** Magical thinking, bizarre beliefs, strange speech or behavior, peculiar dress, perceptual distortions, extreme social discomfort.

### 4. Antisocial Trait
**Characteristics:** Antisocial personality disorder, also known as sociopathy, is the type with the most severe impact on society. All behavior is self-centered, disregarding social norms and the rights of others; it is an abnormal personality that benefits oneself at the expense of others. Actions are unplanned or impulsive; low frustration tolerance, easily irritated, with violent behavior; lack of remorse when harming others, inability to learn from experience.  
**Clues:** Lying, aggressive behavior, criminal history, lack of remorse, repeated rule-breaking, extreme impulsive behavior.

### 5. Borderline Trait
**Characteristics:** Individuals with borderline personality disorder experience a higher degree of emotional distress compared to other personality disorder types, and their symptoms are more complex. It is a psychological abnormality between neurosis and psychosis, hence named "borderline." Main manifestations include: high impulsivity, emotional instability, intense interpersonal relationships, identity disturbance, often accompanied by various impulsive behaviors, persistent feelings of emptiness and boredom, repeated self-harm or suicidal behavior, prone to transient psychotic episodes.

**Characteristics:** Histrionic Personality Disorder, also known as Dramatic Personality Disorder, Hysterical Personality Disorder, or Attention-Seeking Personality Disorder, is characterized by childish behavior, moodiness, affectation, and excessive, unstable emotional expression. Individuals constantly seek attention, often resorting to overly emotional or exaggerated words and actions to attract it. They crave continuous admiration and lack consideration for others.
**Clues:** Attention-seeking, exaggerated language/behavior, rapid mood swings, need to be the center of attention.

### 7. Narcissistic Trait (Narcissistic)
**Characteristics:** Narcissistic Personality Disorder, also referred to as Self-centered Personality Disorder, manifests as childish personality traits with a strong egocentric tendency, often boasting about oneself to attract attention. The core symptom of Narcissistic Personality Disorder is egocentricity. This is specifically manifested as arrogance, exploitativeness, loose and arbitrary thinking, grandiosity, and a tendency to rationalize one's actions. There is a lack of empathy, strong jealousy, and very poor interpersonal relationships.
**Clues:** Exaggerates achievements, needs recognition, extremely sensitive to criticism, disregards others' needs.

### 8. Avoidant Trait (Avoidant)
**Characteristics:** Avoidant Personality Disorder, also known as Anxious Personality Disorder, is characterized by a long-term and pervasive detachment from social relationships. Individuals avoid social interactions, especially occupational activities involving significant interpersonal contact. They are characterized by persistent feelings of tension, apprehension, insecurity, and inferiority. They are overly sensitive in interpersonal relationships, desire acceptance from others, and fear rejection that would hurt their self-esteem, thus avoiding social activities they would otherwise like to participate in due to this internal conflict.
**Clues:** Avoids social situations, reduces contact due to fear of rejection or ridicule, feelings of inferiority.

### 9. Dependent Trait (Dependent)
**Characteristics:** Dependent Personality Disorder is characterized by a lack of self-confidence and independence. Individuals feel helpless, incompetent, and isolated, preferring to place themselves in a subordinate position, letting others make all decisions. Their personality is immature, and they rely on others for help and support in all matters. If forced to act alone, they experience extreme fear.
**Clues:** Strongly seeks caretaking, inability to act independently, fear of losing supporters.

### 10. Obsessive-Compulsive Trait (Obsessive-Compulsive Personality)
**Characteristics:** Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder is characterized by excessive caution, strictness, perfectionism, and inner insecurity. Key features include rigidity, inflexibility in words and actions, adherence to rules and routines, and an inability to adapt flexibly. They struggle to adapt to changing life situations and complex interpersonal relationships. They are excessively doubtful, constantly worrying about unforeseen changes, living in a state of perpetual anxiety. They demand perfection in all things, get bogged down in details, and find it difficult to let go. They are usually overly strict with themselves, lack experiences of pleasure and satisfaction, and are prone to self-blame and regret. They frequently check the details of their work, fearful of making mistakes.
**Clues:** Fixed rituals, strict order, intolerance of mess, excessive focus on rules, rigid daily routines.### 11. Passive-Aggressive Traits (Passive-Aggressive)
**Characteristics:** Passive-aggressive personality disorder expresses strong aggressive tendencies in a passive manner. Outwardly compliant, the individual is uncooperative, stubborn, and procrastinates behind the scenes, obstructing the progress of work. Furthermore, although the person harbors intense aggressive tendencies and hostile feelings internally, they make great efforts to conceal them and dare not show them; while full of complaints, they inwardly strongly desire to depend on authority.
**Clues:** Repeated procrastination, perfunctory behavior, refusal to cooperate without directly expressing dislike.

### 12. Depressive Traits (Depressive Personality)
**Characteristics:** Depressive personality disorder involves a pessimistic outlook, always viewing things negatively; being immersed in a depressive mood; and being prone to worry over minor matters.
**Clues:** Chronic low mood, self-negation, pessimistic language, lack of interest in life.
