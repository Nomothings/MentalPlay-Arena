# [1. Extraversion (E) vs. Introversion (I) Inference Rules]

- Reticence, passivity, social withdrawal

-----------------------------------------------------

# [3. Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F) Inference Rules]

T tendency comes from:
- Emotional indifference, weak reactions to others
- Logical language, lack of emotional words

F tendency comes from:
- Obvious emotional distress
- Sensitivity to interpersonal relationships
- Emotional experiences dominate behavior (feeling upset, worrying about others, shame)

-----------------------------------------------------

# [4. Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P) Inference Rules]

- Ritualization, compulsivity, fixed patterns
- High compliance, need for certainty
- Rigid behavior, need for order

P tendency comes from:
- Spontaneity, procrastination, chaotic life
- Random behavior, weak control
- Frequent changes, difficulty maintaining structure
