Your task is to judge a patient's long-term tendencies (strong / medium / weak) on the two dimensions of the ERQ—Cognitive Reappraisal (CR) and Expressive Suppression (ES)—based on psychiatric medical record text, and provide the reasoning for your inference.

Important: ERQ refers to "emotion regulation style," which is not directly written in medical records. Therefore, your task is not to search for literal content, but to infer through behaviors, emotional reactions, and clinical observations presented in the records.

You must comprehensively infer using the following three types of information:
1. Degree of cooperation during examination (cooperation)
2. Event magnitude vs. strength of emotional reaction (Emotion–Event Mismatch)
3. Emotional expressivity and clinical observation records (Observed Affect)

----------------------------------------------------

[Cognitive Reappraisal (CR) Inference Rules]
CR Strong:
- Major event → Very calm or overly rational
- Emotional distress but forcefully explains "it's fine"
- Clearly suppresses emotions, uses logic to replace feelings
- Superficially stable but internally distressed

- Event reaction is relatively small (emotions suppressed)

CR Medium:
- Sometimes able to think differently, sometimes emotions are too overwhelming to reappraise
- Emotions sometimes regulated by logic, sometimes out of control
- Unstable functioning
- Clinical presentation varies with situation

CR Weak:
- Emotions completely overwhelm thinking, catastrophizing
- Minor event → Major reaction
- Rumination, thinking makes it worse
- Unable to regulate emotions through cognition

----------------------------------------------------

[Expressive Suppression (ES) Inference Rules]

ES Strong:
- Strong emotions but outwardly calm
- Family members say "he/she never expresses emotions"

- Event–emotion mismatch (should be agitated but shows no movement)

ES Medium:
- Suppressive with certain groups (e.g., in front of doctors), but erupts at home
- Unstable ability to control emotions

ES Weak:
- Emotions directly expressed (crying, anger, panic, agitation, impulsivity)
- Expresses suffering in real-time
- Behavior synchronized with emotions
