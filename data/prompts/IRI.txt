The following are the definitions and judgment criteria for the four dimensions:

[1. Perspective Taking (PT)]
Simple Explanation: The ability to see things from another's point of view, understand their emotions and behavioral logic.
Strong: Actively engages in perspective-taking, can understand others' motivations.
Medium: Occasionally takes others' perspectives but inconsistently.
Weak: Difficulty understanding others' feelings, consistently views things from own perspective.

[2. Fantasy Scale (FS)]
Simple Explanation: Whether one empathizes with fictional characters by immersing oneself in movies, novels, or stories.
Strong: Easily immerses in narratives, emotional responses fluctuate with the story.
Medium: Occasionally identifies with characters but not deeply.
Weak: Weak ability to identify, does not immerse in stories.

[3. Empathic Concern (EC)]
Simple Explanation: Whether one feels distress, care, or sympathy for others' suffering.
Strong: Highly sensitive to others' suffering, reactions are pronounced.
Medium: Feels empathy for close others, but not consistently.
Weak: Few reactions, appears indifferent.

[4. Personal Distress (PD)]
Simple Explanation: Whether one experiences anxiety, discomfort, or panic when others are tense or in distress.
Strong: Others' tension → causes strong personal anxiety.
Medium: Experiences discomfort but can regulate it.
Weak: Minimal reaction, remains stable.
