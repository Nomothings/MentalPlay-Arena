# [1. Suicidal Behavior]
**Brief Explanation:**

- **Suicidal Ideation:**

- **Specific Suicide Plan:**
  e.g., "plans to take pills/jump from a building/hang oneself"
- **Past Suicide Attempt:**
  e.g., "overdosed on pills in a suicide attempt," "cut wrists," "attempted jumping from a building"
- **Recurrent Thoughts of Death:**
  e.g., "often thinks about death," "constantly thinks about dying," "wishes to disappear"
